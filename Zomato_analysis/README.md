# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Success Factors Analysis

This project explores the factors that contribute to restaurant success in the Indian food industry using data from Zomato. Through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), data cleaning, feature engineering, and visualization, this study investigates how restaurant characteristics, customer ratings, cuisine diversity, pricing, and service offerings influence restaurant performance.

This project was developed as part of my hands-on learning journey in Data Analytics and demonstrates the application of Python-based analytical techniques to a large-scale real-world dataset.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze restaurant data and identify the key factors associated with restaurant success, customer satisfaction, and market presence.

The analysis focuses on discovering patterns in:

* Customer ratings
* Cuisine diversity
* Pricing strategies
* Restaurant chains
* Service offerings
* Customer sentiment
* Operational characteristics

## 📈 Key Insights

* Restaurants offering a wider variety of cuisines tend to receive slightly higher customer ratings.
* Positive customer sentiments significantly outweigh negative sentiments.
* Major restaurant chains dominate a substantial portion of the market.
* Service features such as takeaway and indoor seating are highly prevalent among successful restaurants.
* Customer ratings exhibit identifiable patterns based on restaurant characteristics and service offerings.
